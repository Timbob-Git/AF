SETUP

Set this up together before the party. The game always uses the lower of the two swap levels for anything between a husband and the other wife. Anything with your own spouse, and the women together, is limited only by the ticks and limits below.

A sip at levels 1–2, two big sips at 3, a shot at 4–5. Saying no to a pairing before the dare is shown is always free, and there's always a "skip free" option.

Use <M1> <M2> <F1> <F2> for the people. Toys, rooms and who touches whom are worked out from the text.
